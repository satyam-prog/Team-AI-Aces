Institutional Privacy Policy & DPDP Compliance

1. Scope & Jurisdiction

This privacy policy governs student education records and academic advisory data managed by EduEagle in accordance with the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023 and FERPA standards.

2. Purpose of Data Processing

Data ingested from Student Information Systems (SIS) and examination ledgers is processed solely to calculate academic early-warning alerts, continuous evaluation marks, and attendance velocity.

3. Role-Based Access Isolation

Student marks, evaluations, and parental summaries are partitioned by institutional role. Students retain strictly read-only visibility into their own individual records.

Terms and Conditions of Institutional Service

1. Authorized Use

Access to EduEagle is restricted to verified university faculty, appointed academic mentors, and registered students.

2. Non-Punitive Mandate

Alerts, risk levels, and attendance flags generated by the platform do not constitute disciplinary sanctions. No student may be subject to examination exclusion solely on the basis of an algorithmic indicator.
