Institutional Privacy Policy & FERPA Compliance

Effective Date: Academic Term 2026-2027 | Governance Authority: Office of the Academic Registrar

1. Scope and Statutory Jurisdiction

This Institutional Privacy Policy defines the operational protocols governing the collection, processing, retention, and dissemination of student education records and behavioral telemetry within the EduEagle Early Warning and Academic Intervention Engine.

EduEagle operates under the strict regulatory governance of the Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act (FERPA), 34 CFR Part 99, and applicable higher education data protection mandates. All recorded student metrics are classified as confidential institutional records.

2. Data Processing Principles and Permitted Purpose

Data ingested from Student Information Systems (SIS), Learning Management Systems (LMS), and departmental examination portals is processed exclusively to calculate actionable early academic warning indicators and assign remedial faculty support.

The following activities are strictly prohibited by institutional policy:

  • Commercial data profiling, advertising syndication, or sale of student records to third parties.
  • Automated academic disqualification, examination exclusion, or disciplinary sanctions based solely on algorithmic outputs.
  • Inference or assignment of clinical medical, psychiatric, or psychological diagnostic labels.

3. Role-Based Data Partitioning and Access Isolation

Access to student dossiers is partitioned strictly by institutional role and functional necessity:

  • Course Instructors: Restricted to academic performance and attendance velocity within their enrolled course sections.
  • Academic Mentors: Access to assigned mentee holistic indicators and scheduled support workflows.
  • Counseling Staff: Confidential student wellness communications and non-clinical support referrals.
  • Bursar & Finance Teams: Financial installment and fee balance records only; zero visibility into coursework evaluations.
  • Students: Isolated self-service access strictly limited to their own verified academic record. Probing peer dossiers is blocked server-side.

4. Student Consent and Self-Determination Rights

Enrolled students maintain statutory rights regarding their educational telemetry:

  • Inspect their calculated risk indicators and factor attribution drivers at any time through the Student Hub.
  • Configure preferences for parental notification, peer tutoring integration, and financial counseling data sharing.
  • Submit direct academic support requests via the Beacon channel without intermediaries.

5. Immutable Audit Trail and Record Retention

Every record modification, risk score recalculation, manual advisor override, and evidence artifact upload is recorded in an immutable institutional audit ledger. Audit entries record the authoritative authenticated user identifier, action timestamp, entity identifier, and operational context.

Records are retained in accordance with university accreditation archiving policies and are purged upon expiration of statutory retention schedules.
