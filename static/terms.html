Terms and Conditions of Institutional Service

Adopted by Institutional Academic Council | Version: 2026.3

1. Authorized Institutional Use

Access to the EduEagle system is granted exclusively to currently appointed university faculty, designated academic mentors, academic administrators, counseling professionals, and registered students. Credentials and session tokens are strictly non-transferable.

2. Non-Punitive Educational Mandate

Risk scoring, early warning flags, and driver attribution metrics generated by the platform are educational advisory signals intended solely to prompt timely academic support, tutorial enrollment, and faculty mentoring.

Under no circumstances shall any automated risk metric or indicator be used to impose academic disqualification, suspend course registration, or penalize a student without independent faculty review and formal departmental inquiry.

3. Non-Diagnostic Medical Disclaimer

EduEagle evaluates academic progress patterns, course evaluation grades, and class attendance velocity. The platform does not diagnose, treat, or provide clinical evaluations for psychological, mental health, or physical medical conditions.

Support requests categorized under wellbeing are referred to licensed campus health and counseling professionals who operate under independent healthcare confidentiality standards.

4. Mandatory Faculty Verification & Service Level Agreements

Appointed advisors and instructors dispatched to an intervention are required to:

  • Independently verify student circumstances through direct consultation prior to confirming or altering intervention status.
  • Adhere to published institutional response Service Level Agreements (SLAs) ranging from 24 to 72 hours based on priority classification.
  • Submit factual, documented outcome evidence (retest attendance logs, signed installment agreements, or advisory notes) before closing an active intervention.

5. Institutional Authority to Override

Authorized faculty advisors and department chairs retain the absolute authority to execute a manual risk override on any student record whenever verified contextual factors supersede automated heuristics. Documented justifications are logged permanently to the institutional audit ledger.
